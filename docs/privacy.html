Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026 • Version 1.0

1. Our Privacy Philosophy

NetGuardian was built on a foundational principle: your network traffic and device telemetry belong solely to you. NetGuardian operates entirely as a local security utility on your Android device. It contains no analytics frameworks, no tracking beacons, no advertising networks, and no telemetry collection systems.

The Core Pledge: NetGuardian never collects, logs, transmits, shares, or sells your personal information, IP addresses, browsing habits, installed apps, or network traffic to any remote server or third party.

2. How the Local Firewall (VpnService) Operates

NetGuardian utilizes the Android VpnService API. Unlike traditional VPN services that forward your Internet traffic to external proxy servers in the cloud, NetGuardian establishes a strictly local loopback TUN interface:

  • Packets are parsed, evaluated against your firewall policies, and forwarded directly to your device's physical Wi-Fi or cellular network adapter.
  • Your packets are never rerouted through remote proxy servers or third-party gateways.
  • All firewall filtering and DNS lookups happen directly on your CPU.

3. Information Handled Strictly on Your Device

To provide firewall security, rule configuration, and real-time telemetry, NetGuardian accesses the following data purely in temporary memory or local on-device SQLite database:

  • Installed Applications List: Scanned locally to let you configure Wi-Fi and Cellular access rules per application. This list never leaves your phone.
  • Connection Logs: Ephemeral destination hostnames, remote IP addresses, ports, and transfer bytes are stored in a local circular buffer (Room DB) on your device. You can purge this log at any time with one tap.
  • User Preferences: Your selected blocked domains, custom upstream DNS providers, and notification toggles are stored in Android encrypted Jetpack DataStore on your device.

4. DNS Queries & External Providers

When you enable DNS filtering, DNS requests are parsed locally. If a domain is blocked, NetGuardian returns a local NXDOMAIN or drop response. If permitted, the query is resolved against the upstream DNS provider you select in Settings (e.g., Cloudflare, Quad9, AdGuard, or your own DNS server). NetGuardian itself operates no DNS servers and records no queries remotely.

5. Permissions & Android System Access

  • BIND_VPN_SERVICE: Required by Android to create the local on-device TUN firewall interface for packet inspection.
  • QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES: Scans installed packages locally to configure per-app Wi-Fi and Cellular blocking rules across all applications. Package inventory is never transmitted off your device.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE & FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE: Keeps the local packet filtering engine operating reliably in the background with persistent notification visibility.
  • INTERNET: Required to send permitted traffic and resolve upstream DNS lookups.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Detects whether your active connection is Wi-Fi or Cellular to apply your custom firewall rules.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Displays the active firewall status notification and Quick Action toggles.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED: Automatically starts firewall protection on phone reboot (optional, controlled by user).

6. Third-Party Libraries & Zero Analytics

NetGuardian uses only essential, open-source Android Jetpack and Kotlin libraries. We do not integrate Google Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, Facebook SDK, Adjust, AppsFlyer, or any commercial ad network.

7. Open Source & Auditability

NetGuardian is 100% open source under the Apache 2.0 License. You can audit every line of code, build pipeline, and network handler directly on GitHub at github.com/memamun/NetGuardian.
