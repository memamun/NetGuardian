Security & Control

Engineered for Absolute Device Autonomy

No subscriptions, no cloud proxies, and no complex configurations. Pure on-device packet filtering.

No-Root Local TUN Architecture

Creates a local Linux TUN loopback interface on Android using VpnService. Evaluates every socket frame directly on your CPU without routing traffic through any remote server.

Granular Per-App Controls

Stop apps from secretly phoning home or draining your mobile data bundle. Individually toggle independent rules for Wi-Fi and Cellular data for each installed app.

DNS Interceptor & Ad Shield

Intercepts port 53 DNS lookups, cross-references queries against your local blacklist, and securely forwards permitted lookups to Cloudflare, Quad9, or your custom DNS provider.

Circular Room DB Logging

Inspect destination IP addresses, hostnames, timestamps, and total transferred bytes. Automatically purges old entries in an efficient circular buffer to protect device storage.

Quick Settings Tiles

Toggle firewall states directly from your Android notification tray without opening the app: Main Firewall, Block All Traffic, Wi-Fi Only, and Mobile Data Only tiles.

Modern Material 3 Expressive

Polished with Google's latest Material 3 Expressive guidelines: dynamic system coloring, edge-to-edge system bars, OLED deep blacks, and full Android 14+ compatibility.

System Design

How Local Packet Interception Works

Conventional VPN services route your data to servers in Virginia or Frankfurt where it can be logged. NetGuardian runs the entire firewall pipeline on your handset.

1

Virtual TUN Interface

Android routes outbound sockets through the local NetGuardian TUN device descriptor.

2

Packet Parser & Policy Engine

NetGuardian extracts IPv4/IPv6 headers and cross-checks the UID against your Wi-Fi and Cellular policy table.

3

Direct Local Forwarding

Permitted packets are dispatched directly over your device's actual network interface with zero latency penalty.
